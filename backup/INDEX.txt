===============================================
  VERIFICACION DE INTEGRIDAD
===============================================

Después de crear el backup, ejecuta:

powershell -Command "Get-ChildItem backup -Recurse -File | Where-Object {$_.Extension -in '.exe','.whl','.pth','.zip'} | Select-Object FullName,Length | Format-Table -AutoSize"

Esto mostrará todos los archivos importantes y sus tamaños.

===============================================
  CHECKSUMS (para verificar integridad)
===============================================

Genera checksums con:

powershell -Command "Get-ChildItem backup -Recurse -File | Where-Object {$_.Extension -in '.exe','.whl','.pth','.zip'} | Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 | Select-Object Path,Hash | Export-Csv backup_checksums.csv"

Guarda backup_checksums.csv junto con el backup para verificar
integridad al restaurar o distribuir.
